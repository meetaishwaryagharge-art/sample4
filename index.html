How It's Applied

Four Coats.
Zero Guesswork.

Every Ominex wall system follows the same sequence on site — from bare plaster to a 5-year finish.

01
Prime the surface

Apply Eco Prime / Interior / Exterior Primer to seal plaster and even out absorption.

02
Fill with putty

Two coats of Acrylic Putty for a pore-free, roller-smooth base — sand once dry.

03
Coat with emulsion

Finish with 3X Exterior or OmineXpert Emulsion in your chosen shade, two coats.

04
Protect exposed areas

Terraces, parapets & damp-prone walls get a Damp Protect waterproof layer.
